# Michael Rasmussen: 2017 draft, scouting projections, peers, and "bust"/re-draft discussion

Compiled 2026-09-08.

## Draft-year facts
- 2016-17, Tri-City Americans (WHL): 50 GP 32-23-55, team-best 15 PPG (tied 6th in WHL); broken wrist mid-February 2017, surgery, missed the rest of the season (20 games). Listed 6'5"-6'6", 205-221 lb. (Wikipedia citing NHL.com Canucks prospect profile, 2017-06-06; NHL.com Red Wings, 2017-06-24)
- Rankings: NHL Central Scouting No. 5 North American skater (final); ISS 8; McKeen's 3; Future Considerations 18. (WIIM draft profile, Kyle Bauer, https://www.wingingitinmotown.com/2017-red-wings-draft-profile-michael-rasmussen-offers-size-and-skill-red-wings-covet/, 2017-05-12; Wikipedia)
- Craig Button (TSN) described him as "a solid player with size that can score at the net." (Wikipedia citing TSN, 2017-04-10)
- Selected 9th overall, June 23, 2017, United Center, Chicago. GM Ken Holland; director of amateur scouting Tyler Wright. Detroit's next picks: Gustav Lindstrom at 38 (Jason Robertson went 39 to Dallas). (NHL.com; DSN 2026-09-05)

## What he was projected to be (verbatim)
- Detroit News, draft night (Ted Kulfan, https://www.detroitnews.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2017/06/23/red-wings-pick-big-forward-rasmussen-draft/103154624/, 2017-06-23; body paywalled, snippet): "Many scouts feel he's a prototype power forward, with an inclination to drive to the net, and should become a top-six forward in the NHL." Headline: "He's 'all business': Red Wings draft big center Michael Rasmussen."
- Tyler Wright, 2017-06-24: "This guy is all business. He's a very mature young man that lives and breathes and dies hockey... we want to be a harder team to play against, we want to get bigger down the middle and he just kind of checked all those boxes." / "We had those three guys targeted and we weren't moving down. We actually had an opportunity to move down. We decided to make the pick." / "he's big and he can skate and he can think." Wright pictured Rasmussen, Mantha and Svechnikov's size plus Larkin and Athanasiou's speed as the future core. (NHL.com Red Wings, https://www.nhl.com/redwings/news/wings-tyler-wright-liked-michael-rasmussen-on-and-off-the-ice-290111390)
- Free Press draft-day scouting report (recapped 2018-06-16, https://www.freep.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2018/06/16/detroit-red-wings-michael-rasmussen/707509002/): "Goes to the hard areas. Plays the net-front on the power play. Good scoring touch..."
- WIIM profile (Bauer, 2017-05-12): NHL comparable Corey Perry. Strengths: "large frame effectively deployed attacking the net," "unquestioned finishing touch on pucks" (50 goals in 114 WHL games), redirects/rebounds in high-danger areas, PP specialist, aggressive forechecker, "smooth skater with decent touch and vision." Weaknesses: defensive awareness, faceoffs, "all around game seems lacking to be a top-six center," may be primarily a "power play weapon" dependent on linemates, "struggles driving playmaking consistently as a center." Projection: "a good middle-six forward" rather than an elite scorer, "better suited as a winger than center." Quote: "I see a power forward all the way, who muscles around down low. He has an unquestioned finishing touch on pucks."
- Future Considerations (via Elite Prospects, cited by Octopus Thrower 2026-03-11): praised his ability to "block the goaltender's view."
- First Star Scouting (cited by DSN 2026-09-05): "a big, responsible two-way center who was a nightmare down low. A 6-foot-6, 200-foot player."
- Detroit Hockey Now, 2025-10-23 (Bob Duff): "When the Detroit Red Wings made Michael Rasmussen the ninth overall selection of the 2017 NHL entry draft, many hockey people cringed. They didn't see the chance that Rasmussen would be developing into an elite NHL performer worthy of a top-10 selection on draft day."
- Reddit reaction on draft night: thread "The Detroit Red Wings select Michael Rasmussen with the 9th overall pick" (https://www.reddit.com/r/DetroitRedWings/comments/6j517x/, 2017-06-23); content not retrievable.
- The 30-goal question: THW's July 2022 retrospective framed the pick as "a chance at a franchise centerman"; the WIIM profile explicitly projected middle-six rather than elite scorer. A Reddit fan in June 2023 called him a "Future 30 goal scorer" (https://www.reddit.com/r/DetroitRedWings/comments/14729st/). No pre-draft source found that projected 30 NHL goals; the "he should score at 6'6" expectation is fan-generated and rests on his 63 WHL goals in his last 97 junior games (THN, 2026-08-06).

## Post-draft development path
- 2017-18: returned to Tri-City as captain; 47 GP 31-28-59; 14 playoff GP 16-17-33. (CapWages; NHL.com 2024-02-20 release)
- 2018-19: made Detroit at 19 straight from junior (Wright had said "all these kids are a little bit naive... they think that they can make this next step right away"); 62 GP 8-10-18; Blashill: "I think he's going to score lots of goals in this league because he knows how to go to the net." (HockeyBuzz/Duff 2018-11-17)
- 2019-20: full season in the AHL at 20, back injury, 35 GP 7-15-22; no NHL games.
- 2020-21: Graz 99ers loan (18 GP 16 pts), taxi squad, 40 NHL GP 3-9-12.
- 2021-22: first full NHL season, 80 GP 15-12-27; moved to wing by Blashill. THW July 2022: "While the Detroit Red Wings likely hoped for more, Michael Rasmussen has the toolkit to be a solid bottom-six forward for the franchise... This may not be an ideal use of a top-ten pick, but it's at least not a total loss either." (https://thehockeywriters.com/2017-nhl-entry-draft-top-10-where-are-they-now/, 2022-07-07)
- 2022-23 / 2023-24: peak, 29 and 33 points; bottom-six checking role with PK; 4-year extension.
- 2024-25 / 2025-26: 21 and 14 points; "utility guy" at 12-13 minutes.
- Career through 2025-26: 454 GP 66-88-154. Rasmussen, Feb 2024: "There's been ups and downs in my career." Would be the last Ken Holland draft pick on the roster if Larkin is traded (DSN, 2026-09-05).

- THN, 2026-08-06 (Whitaker): the pick "set the franchise back years"; "another notch in a series of complete misses by former GM Ken Holland and Amateur Scouting Director Tyler Wright"; Tippett, Necas, Suzuki, Thomas, Robertson and Norris "have made significant contributions since entering the NHL, leaps and bounds ahead of what Rasmussen has provided for Detroit." "Although he scored a combined 63 goals in 97 games over his final two seasons with the WHL's Tri-City Americans, that offensive production has never translated to the NHL." Placed alongside the 2018 Zadina-over-Quinn Hughes miss.
- THN re-draft, 2026-08-21: Detroit at 9 takes Owen Tippett; Rasmussen is not selected in the re-drafted top 10. Rationale: "Detroit is missing consistency throughout its lineup, and Tippett provides a high motor with great skill, especially off the rush."

- Detroit Sports Nation, 2026-09-05: "At the time, drafting Rasmussen seemed like an easy choice; he was big, physical, and played a desirable position at center. Looking back now, in hindsight, is painful, to say the least. Especially when Detroit spent the 38th pick on Gustav Lindstrom, one selection before Dallas took Jason Robertson at 39th... It's easy to judge in hindsight, but even so, it's a decision that gets worse with age."

## "Bust" discussion, and the counter-argument
- Bust camp: r/DetroitRedWings "Michael Rasmussen is a bust... doesn't have the offensive skillset to ever justify his draft position" (approx. July 2026); "How in hell do your 1sts from 2015-2018 bust so bad?" (Sept 2023); THN commenter: "One of the worst draft selections that Ken Holland ever made" (Mar 2026); DHN: "The Rasmussen experiment has clearly run its course" (Oct 2025).
- The role-player defense: DHN, Jan 2024: "When you're a top-10 selection in your draft year, people anticipate the spectacular. However, spectacular is not part of Rasmussen's hockey DNA. Solid? Reliable? Dependable? Trustworthy? Yes." WIIM, Feb 2024: "there were other prospects on the board at pick nine that perhaps most fans would prefer today, but Rasmussen has made the case for himself over the past few seasons as a viable option on any line and in any situation." Reddit, Sept 2023: "You don't always get a great player at 9." Inside The Rink, Feb 2024: "Any NHL general manager would gladly take this type of player if you could guarantee that they were drafted with the ninth overall pick."
- The "Holland era" framing: DSN, Sept 2026: if Larkin is traded, "Rasmussen will be the last player drafted by Ken Holland who still plays for Detroit... I would think of it like the last cleansing, the new start."
- Ceiling comps offered by media: Nicolas Roy (30-40 point two-way C, traded for a conditional 1st) per THN 2026; Mason Appleton ($300K cheaper, similar output) per Octopus Thrower 2026; Adam Lowry contract comp per THW 2024.
