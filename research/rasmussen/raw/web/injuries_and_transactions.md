# Michael Rasmussen: injuries, transactions, contracts (2017 draft to Sept 8, 2026)

Compiled 2026-09-08. Primary sources preferred (team site, beat writers, Detroit Hockey Now, sportsforecaster transaction log, CapWages). PuckPedia is behind a Cloudflare JS wall to curl; CapWages fetched cleanly. The sportsforecaster transactions log (https://sportsforecaster.com/nhl/p/26779/Michael_Rasmussen/transactions, fetched 2026-09-08) is the backbone for exact dates and games missed; each entry below is cross-checked against a beat-writer source where one exists.

Bio anchors: born April 17, 1999, Vancouver, BC (raised in Surrey); 6'6", 220-222 lb; shoots L; #27. Drafted 9th overall by Detroit, June 23, 2017 (Ken Holland GM, Tyler Wright director of amateur scouting). (Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Rasmussen_(ice_hockey), rev. 2026-08-07; NHL.com Red Wings, https://www.nhl.com/redwings/news/wings-tyler-wright-liked-michael-rasmussen-on-and-off-the-ice-290111390, 2017-06-24)

## Dated timeline

### Junior / draft year
- 2014: selected 7th overall by Tri-City (WHL) in the WHL bantam draft. (Wikipedia)
- 2016-17 (draft year): broken wrist in mid-February 2017 that required surgery; missed the rest of the season; limited to 50 of 70 games. 32-23-55, team-best 15 PP goals (tied 6th in WHL). Ranked No. 5 North American skater by NHL Central Scouting. (NHL.com Canucks prospect profile by Ryan Biech, 2017-06-06, cited via Wikipedia: "Rasmussen's year ended early as he suffered a broken wrist in the middle of February that required surgery and thus missed the remainder of the season.") Tyler Wright on draft night: "he had an injury, got hurt in January, a little bit misdiagnosed and didn't play after middle of February. So a little bit out of sight, out of mind kind of helped us in our favor." (NHL.com, 2017-06-24, URL above)
- June 23, 2017: drafted 9th overall. (NHL.com Red Wings, https://www.nhl.com/redwings/news/center-michael-rasmussen-selected-with-ninth-overall-pick-of-2017-nhl--290097086)
- Aug 5, 2017: signed 3-year entry-level contract. CapWages lists total value $5,225,000 incl. bonuses, cap hit $894,166, signed by Ken Holland. (CapWages, https://capwages.com/players/michael-rasmussen, fetched 2026-09-08; NHL.com, https://www.nhl.com/redwings/news/michael-rasmussen-takes-first-step-toward-becoming-a-red-wing-290602444)
- Oct 1, 2017: returned to Tri-City (WHL); ELC slid one year (2017-18 "ENTRY-LEVEL SLIDE" on CapWages). 2017-18 WHL: 47 GP 31-28-59 as captain; 14 playoff GP 16-17-33. (sportsforecaster; CapWages)

### 2018-19 (rookie NHL season, age 19; coach Jeff Blashill)
- Oct 4, 2018: NHL debut vs Columbus. (Wikipedia; NHL.com)
- Oct 15, 2018: healthy scratch at Montreal (first of his career), with the 10-game ELC threshold looming. Blashill: "Raz is a real good player and he's going to be a good player, but as a young player, sometimes it's good to take a breather." (Detroit News, Ted Kulfan, https://www.detroitnews.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2018/10/15/rookie-michael-rasmussen-odd-man-out-blashill-shakes-up-lineup/1646937002/, 2018-10-15). Detroit kept him past 10 games; first ELC year burned.
- Oct 30, 2018: first NHL goal vs CBJ. (Wikipedia)
- Jan 4, 2019: last game before hamstring problem. Jan 6 lower-body day-to-day; Jan 15 placed on IR (hamstring). Jan 30 assigned to Grand Rapids for conditioning; Feb 7 recalled. Missed 10 NHL games. AHL conditioning stint: 3 GP 2-0-2. (sportsforecaster; Detroit News, Kulfan, https://www.detroitnews.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2019/02/04/michael-rasmussens-time-grand-rapids-griffins-brief-but-good-him/2767943002/, 2019-02-04: "Rasmussen had been bothered by hamstring issues and hadn't played since Jan. 4.")
- Mar 23, 2019: upper-body injury; missed the last 8 regular-season games. (sportsforecaster, entry dated 2019-04-06)
- Season line: 62 GP 8-10-18, 12:05 TOI, -8. (CapWages)

### 2019-20 (zero NHL games; back injury in Grand Rapids)
- Sept 29, 2019: assigned to Grand Rapids (AHL) out of camp. (sportsforecaster) Fan-thread title from that month: "Red Wings Want Michael Rasmussen at Center" (r/DetroitRedWings, https://www.reddit.com/r/DetroitRedWings/comments/d4zi3d/red_wings_want_michael_rasmussen_at_center/, Sept 2019).
- Nov 12, 2019: last Griffins game before injury. Out with "an undisclosed injury but believed to be back related" (MLive, https://www.mlive.com/griffins/2020/01/michael-rasmussen-plays-with-griffins-for-first-time-since-nov-12.html, Jan 2020, headline: "Michael Rasmussen plays with Griffins for first time since Nov. 12"). The Athletic (Max Bultman, 2020-01-16, https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/1538180/2020/01/16/back-from-injury-michael-rasmussen-is-ready-to-resume-a-key-season-in-grand-rapids/, paywalled; deck reads "After missing two months with a back injury, the former first-rounder can get back to honing his defensive game at center").
- Missed 23 consecutive AHL games (mid-Nov 2019 to mid-Jan 2020). Griffins game notes, Jan 22, 2020: "Second-year pro Michael Rasmussen has collected four points (2-2-4) in his first five games back in the lineup after missing the previous 23 due to injury." (https://griffinshockey.com/news/game-notes-griffins-vs-wild-jan-22-2020)
- Season line: 35 GP 7-15-22 with Grand Rapids; the AHL season was cut short by COVID in March 2020. Detroit Jock City, 2020-04-13: "injuries to his back kept him off the ice this season." (https://detroitjockcity.com/2020/04/13/detroit-red-wings-michael-rasmussen/)
- No surgery reported for the back; no source found saying otherwise.

### 2020-21 (Graz loan, taxi squad, AHL; final ELC year)
- Sept 23, 2020: loaned to Graz 99ers (ICEHL, Austria) because of the delayed NHL start. (Detroit News, https://www.detroitnews.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2020/09/23/red-wings-loan-forward-michael-rasmussen-austrian-team/3511407001/; team X post 2020-09-23, https://x.com/DetroitRedWings/status/1308877350935949320). Graz: 18 GP 5-11-16, 42 PIM.
- Dec 22, 2020: recalled from Graz. (sportsforecaster)
- Jan 13 - Apr 8, 2021: shuttled between NHL roster and taxi squad at least eight times (Jan 13, Jan 18, Jan 29, Jan 30, Feb 1, Mar 7, Mar 9, Apr 2, Apr 3, Apr 4, Apr 6, Apr 7, Apr 8). (sportsforecaster)
- Feb 1, 2021: assigned to Grand Rapids (7 GP 2-4-6). Mar 2, 2021: emergency recall. Mar 5: reassigned. (sportsforecaster; NHL.com, https://www.nhl.com/redwings/news/red-wings-assign-michael-rasmussen-to-grand-rapids-322139504, noting he was "limited to 35 games due to injury" in 2019-20)
- NHL line: 40 GP 3-9-12, 14:48 TOI, -5. (CapWages)
- July 22, 2021: signed 3-year, $4.38M extension ($1.46M AAV; salaries $1.0M/$1.48M/$1.9M), expiry status RFA with arbitration rights. Signed by Steve Yzerman. (CapWages; NHL.com, https://www.nhl.com/redwings/news/red-wings-extend-michael-rasmussen-for-three-season-325736538)

### 2021-22 (Blashill's last year; moved to wing)
- Dec 15, 2021: COVID-19 protocol; missed 2 games. (sportsforecaster)
- Jan 2022: played several games at wing with Pius Suter and Robby Fabbri. (Detroit News, Kulfan, https://www.detroitnews.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2022/01/13/detroit-red-wings-michael-rasmussen-shows-versatility-playing-wing/6510901001/, 2022-01-13)
- Late Feb 2022: Blashill moved Joe Veleno to 3C with Rasmussen on his wing. "The negative of that move is that Rasmussen has to go to the wing and the Red Wings see him as big checking center." (DHN, Kevin Allen, https://detroithockeynow.com/detroit-red-wings-first-round-pick-joe-veleno-needs-to-show-he-can-be-scoring-center/, 2022-02-28)
- Season line: 80 GP 15-12-27 (career-high goals), 14:31, -25, 66 PIM. (CapWages)
- Summer 2022: Blashill out; Derek Lalonde hired as head coach.

### 2022-23 (Lalonde year 1; suspension, then knee injury ends season)
- Oct 27, 2022: high-stick to David Krejci's head in Boston. Oct 28: suspended 2 games by NHL Player Safety; forfeited $15,784. Missed 2 games. (NHL.com, https://www.nhl.com/news/detroit-red-wings-michael-rasmussen-suspended-336923284; Detroit News, https://www.detroitnews.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2022/10/28/detroit-red-wings-michael-rasmussen-suspended-two-games-for-high-sticking/69601124007/, 2022-10-28)
- Feb 25, 2023: blocked a Zach Bogosian shot with his kneecap vs Tampa Bay. Feb 26: "Out indefinitely" (Lalonde: "We will not see him anytime soon"). Mar 2: placed on IR (knee); Lalonde said he was likely done for the season. Missed the last 24 regular-season games. THN later described it as "a broken kneecap." (DHN, Kevin Allen, https://detroithockeynow.com/2023/02/26/michael-rasmussen-out-indefinitely-took-shot-off-kneecap-detroit-red-wings/, 2023-02-26; Ansar Khan X post 2023-03-02, https://x.com/AnsarKhanMLive/status/1631332528425500672; sportsforecaster; THN, https://thehockeynews.com/nhl/detroit-red-wings/players/todd-mclellan-confident-michael-rasmussen-will-respond-the-right-way-after-scratch, 2025-10-26). No surgery reported.
- Season line: 56 GP 10-19-29 (career highs in A and P at the time), 15:06, +2. Detroit went 8-16-2 in the 26 games he missed (2 suspension + 24 injury). (CapWages; DHN, https://detroithockeynow.com/2024/04/03/red-wings-will-miss-rasmussen-lalonde-fischer-copp/, 2024-04-03)

### 2023-24 (career year; extension; late upper-body injury)
- Feb 20, 2024: signed 4-year, $12.8M extension, $3.2M AAV, running 2024-25 through 2027-28; expiry status UFA (summer 2028). Salary structure per CapWages: 2024-25 $2.05M + $500K signing bonus; 2025-26 $2.95M; 2026-27 $3.65M; 2027-28 $3.65M. No trade protection listed (CapWages "Clause" column blank for all four years; ClutchPoints noted "no trade protection clauses limiting team flexibility"). Signed by Yzerman. NOTE: CapWages labels this contract "Two-Way Contract (Extension)"; no outlet reported it as a two-way deal and the salary figures are NHL-level, so treat that label as a site quirk. (CapWages; NHL.com press release, https://www.nhl.com/redwings/news/red-wings-sign-michael-rasmussen-to-four-year-contract-extension, 2024-02-20; ESPN, https://www.espn.com/nhl/story/_/id/39563760/red-wings-michael-rasmussen-signs-4-year-128m-extension; ClutchPoints, https://clutchpoints.com/nhl/detroit-red-wings/red-wings-grading-michael-rasmussen-contract, 2024-02-21)
  - Correction to the brief: the deal was signed in February 2024, not July 2023. At signing he had 23 pts (11-12) in 55 GP and led Detroit forwards at +11; he would have been an RFA that summer. (NHL.com, 2024-02-20 and 2024-02-21)
- Apr 1, 2024: upper-body injury in a board collision late in a 4-2 win at Tampa Bay. Apr 3: ruled out, day-to-day (Lalonde). Missed the final 7 regular-season games; had not missed a game before that. (DHN, 2024-04-03, URL above; sportsforecaster entry 2024-04-17)
- Season line: 75 GP 13-20-33 (career highs in A and P), 15:11 (career-high TOI), +8. (CapWages)

### 2024-25 (Lalonde fired Dec 26; McLellan hired)
- Nov 16, 2024: upper-body, day-to-day; missed 1 game. (sportsforecaster)
- Dec 26, 2024: Lalonde fired; Todd McLellan named head coach. (NHL.com, https://www.nhl.com/news/todd-mclellan-named-detroit-coach-replacing-fired-derek-lalonde) Rasmussen: 12 pts in 33 GP under Lalonde, 9 pts in 44 GP under McLellan (RotoWire summary via search).
- Jan 25, 2025: scored an ENG and was shaken up on a play involving Kucherov; McLellan indicated he "appears to be okay." (DHN, https://detroithockeynow.com/2025/01/25/red-wings-room-mclellan-indicates-rasmussen-appears-to-be-okay-mclellan-talbot/; r/DetroitRedWings thread "Rasmussen ENG and injury 01/25/2025")
- Feb 23, 2025: took a high hit from Anaheim's Trevor Zegras ("blow to the head" per Free Press); no penalty called; Zegras suspended 3 games. Feb 24: day-to-day. Missed 4 games. At the time he "centered the third line, played net-front on the second power-play unit and helped out killing penalties." (Detroit Free Press, Helene St. James, https://www.freep.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2025/02/24/detroit-red-wings-injury-news-michael-rasmussen-andrew-copp/80040456007/, 2025-02-24; sportsforecaster entry 2025-03-05)
- Season line: 77 GP 11-10-21, 13:25, -12; 2nd among Detroit forwards in hits (141) and blocks (59). (CapWages; DHN, https://detroithockeynow.com/red-wings-review-michael-rasmussen/, 2025-05-10)

### 2025-26 (McLellan's first full season; scratches, three injury stints)
- Oct 25, 2025: first healthy scratch of the season (vs St. Louis; Detroit dressed 11F/7D). Scratched twice during the season. (THN, 2025-10-26, URL above; DHN, https://detroithockeynow.com/red-wings-report-card-rasmussen-in-regression/, 2026-05-30)
- Nov 20-26, 2025: undisclosed injury; placed on IR Nov 26 retroactive to Nov 20; missed 3 games; returned Dec 3. (sportsforecaster; CBS Sports, https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/hockey/news/red-wings-michael-rasmussen-lands-on-injured-reserve/)
- Mar 12, 2026: injured (shot to the leg) in a 4-1 loss to Tampa Bay. Mar 14: McLellan says out at least a week. Mar 24: week-to-week. Missed 9 games. Returned Apr 4 at NY Rangers. (MLive, Ansar Khan, https://www.mlive.com/redwings/2026/03/red-wings-hampered-by-more-injuries-to-forwards.html, 2026-03-14; Daily Faceoff, https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/news/detroit-red-wings-provide-injury-updates-michael-rasmussen-brandsegg-nygard-nhl-news, 2026-03-14; CBS Sports "Good to return Saturday," 2026-04-04; sportsforecaster)
- Apr 7, 2026: in his third game back, blocked a Zach Werenski shot "in the exact same spot" on the leg during a CBJ power play; did not return. Apr 9: ruled out for the remainder of the regular season (leg). Missed the final 4 games. McLellan: "he blocked the shot basically in the exact same spot, so I don't know if he's starting over or not." (DHN, Bob Duff, https://detroithockeynow.com/red-wings-rasmussen-done-for-season/, 2026-04-08; Daily Faceoff, https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/news/detroit-red-wings-michael-rasmussen-leaves-game-columbus-blue-jackets-lower-body-injury-nhl-news, 2026-04-07; sportsforecaster entries 2026-04-09 and 2026-04-17). No surgery reported.
- Season line: 64 GP 6-8-14, -10, 8 PIM, 12:39 TOI (lowest since rookie year). Pointless in his last 25 games; 1 goal in his last 30. Led Detroit forwards with 63 blocked shots. (CapWages; DHN 2026-04-08 and 2026-05-30)
- Detroit missed the playoffs for the 10th straight year.
