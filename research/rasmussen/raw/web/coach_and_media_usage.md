# Michael Rasmussen: how coaches and management use him, and what media/analytics have said

Compiled 2026-09-08. Quotes are verbatim from the linked source.

## Part 1: Coaches and management

### Ken Holland / Tyler Wright era (draft, 2017)
- Tyler Wright (director of amateur scouting), draft night: "we want to be a harder team to play against, we want to get bigger down the middle and he just kind of checked all those boxes." / "It was an area of need through the middle with size and competitiveness, with some ability to finish." / "I had zero issues with his skating... he's big and he can skate and he can think." (NHL.com Red Wings, Dana Wakiji, https://www.nhl.com/redwings/news/wings-tyler-wright-liked-michael-rasmussen-on-and-off-the-ice-290111390, 2017-06-24)

### Jeff Blashill (2018-19 to 2021-22)
- On keeping the 19-year-old and scratching him once (Oct 15, 2018): "Raz is a real good player and he's going to be a good player, but as a young player, sometimes it's good to take a breather." / "Out West it wasn't quite as quick, it was more of a big man's game and he excelled. The last two have been quick, and he has to learn to make sure he's skating the second he has the puck, or people are on top of him quick." / "Raz is going to be a real good NHL player, I have tons of belief in him." (Detroit News, Ted Kulfan, https://www.detroitnews.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2018/10/15/rookie-michael-rasmussen-odd-man-out-blashill-shakes-up-lineup/1646937002/, 2018-10-15)
- Net-front projection, Nov 17, 2018 (Rasmussen had 5 goals in 10 games, 3 on the PP, leading the team in PPG): "He's an excellent net-front guy and I think he's a good player everywhere else. So where is a guy going to separate himself in the NHL? To me, it's net front for him. I thought that when I watched him last year. I think he's going to score lots of goals in this league because he knows how to go to the net, he knows how to tip pucks, he knows how to screen, he knows how to leverage his body. I think he's going to be a real good net-front guy for a long time." Mike Green: "Ras has done a good job as a young guy getting his big body in front." Jimmy Howard: "With Ras being out there, it creates a lot of havoc for the goalies because you can't really look over him." (Bob Duff, HockeyBuzz, https://www.hockeybuzz.com/2025/07/18/Rasmatazz, originally published 2018-11-17)
- Dec 20, 2018: "Red Wings' Blashill pleased with Michael Rasmussen's development, despite growing pains... no one is particularly disappointed with the way Rasmussen has played." (Detroit News, https://www.detroitnews.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2018/12/20/detroit-red-wings-michael-rasmussen-jeff-blashill/2371731002/; body paywalled beyond the lede)
- Center vs. wing, Feb 28, 2022 (Veleno moved to 3C, Rasmussen to his wing): "Does (moving Rasmussen to wing) free him up a little bit more to be net front? Maybe. That was a conversation we've had internally for a year, is can he be a real good net-front guy and a center, when you're work is to get back first all the time. Does it free that up a little bit?" And: "They're both learning how to be a good defensive center in the league. I would say in the end they'll both end up in the middle of the ice." Writer's framing: "the Red Wings see him as big checking center." (DHN, Kevin Allen, https://detroithockeynow.com/detroit-red-wings-first-round-pick-joe-veleno-needs-to-show-he-can-be-scoring-center/, 2022-02-28)
- Jan 13, 2022 (Kulfan): "Chances are, playing the 6-foot-6 Rasmussen in the middle anchoring defensive-minded lines is where the Wings see Rasmussen long term... But Rasmussen has played wing... with Pius Suter and Robby Fabbri, Rasmussen provided size, net-front presence and chipped in offensively." (Detroit News, https://www.detroitnews.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2022/01/13/detroit-red-wings-michael-rasmussen-shows-versatility-playing-wing/6510901001/)
- Inside The Rink's summary of the era: "Under former head coach Jeff Blashill, Rasmussen moved from center to the left wing. This was to be able to stick on the Red Wings' lineup, something that was in question just two seasons ago." (Andrew Walsh, https://insidetherink.com/what-the-michael-rasmussen-signing-means-for-the-detroit-red-wings/, 2024-02-24)

### Derek Lalonde (2022-23 to Dec 26, 2024)
- Dec 2022 (Octopus Thrower): Lalonde "trusts [Rasmussen] as a top-checking center late in games." 5v5 CF% 47.4, 1.5 P/60 at 5v5, both career highs at the time. (https://octopusthrower.com/2022/12/19/michael-rasmussen-valuable-red-wings/, 2022-12-19)
- Feb 26, 2023, after the kneecap injury: "Huge, obviously the way he was playing... The pride and the approach him and Copp have on the penalty kill. We were in a situation where we were only using four killers. They were killing almost two-thirds of every kill we had." Writer: "He is one of the team's most valuable performers because he plays among the top six forwards, offers a physical game and is a strong penalty killer." PK was 34-for-38 (89.5%) since Jan 17. (DHN, Kevin Allen, https://detroithockeynow.com/2023/02/26/michael-rasmussen-out-indefinitely-took-shot-off-kneecap-detroit-red-wings/)
- Jan 10, 2024 ("Rasmussen is who he is... and that's not a bad thing"): "Consistency, hard to play against. When he's at his best loves to pitch in on offense. His physicality. Whoever he's with, he seems to drive them because he's able to give them looks. He'll retrieve pucks on forechecks and plays a predictable, simple game. He's easy to play off." / "His feet, his legs, having his legs in there gets some extra touches, creates off the forecheck. A very valuable player for us. It's always a bonus when he's able to contribute offensively." / "I think Ras been really good within his role. We've asked him to kill (penalties). We ask him to check at times. When you start adding on to some of these players it takes away from what they're really good at." Duff's framing: "he's not going to be a regular player among Detroit's top-six forwards. And he isn't going to be a net-front presence for the team's power play." Stats cited: 51.6% O-zone faceoffs, 2nd among F in hits (41), led F in blocks (44), fewest giveaways/60 on roster (0.49), +7, 2:01 SH TOI/game (3rd among F). (DHN, Bob Duff, https://detroithockeynow.com/2024/01/10/red-wings-rasmussen-is-who-he-is-lalonde/)
- Feb 22, 2024 (extension week): "You see the way I use him. The staff is a fan of him, know he's a valuable player. Last month alone, he centered a quality fourth line, he's been on a checking line, we put him on our top line to give us a spark. Obviously, he kills for us." / "A good pro, a good human, a good individual. Really happy for him... We've improved as a group the last couple of years and he's been a big part of it." (DHN, Bob Duff, https://detroithockeynow.com/2024/02/22/red-wings-rasmussen-a-true-team-player-lalonde-copp-fischer/) Same week, NHL.com: "Red Wings head coach Derek Lalonde has repeatedly praised Rasmussen for his versatility and work ethic." J.T. Compher: "He plays a big part of our team and can do a lot of different things. When he's on and using his body and protecting pucks, he can be a dominant player out there." (NHL.com Red Wings, Jonathan Mills, https://www.nhl.com/redwings/news/i-love-it-here-rasmussen-grateful-for-four-year-extension-with-red-wings, 2024-02-21)
- Mar 5, 2024 (on Veleno as a "five-in-one tool"): "(Andrew) Copp is similar like that. (Michael Rasmussen) is similar. I think extremely valuable, especially in today's NHL." (DHN, Kevin Allen, https://detroithockeynow.com/joe-veleno-dylan-larkin-injury-replacement-swiss-army-knife-detroit-red-wings/)
- Apr 3, 2024, after the upper-body injury (Copp-Rasmussen-Fischer "identity line"): "We've had a little success there getting that line back together, him, Copp and Fisch, eats up really good minutes... Maybe the way we're built, he gives us some hard, and some size. He's around the net, he's helping drive his line off the forecheck. That will be a miss for us. We'll feel that a little bit." (DHN, Bob Duff, https://detroithockeynow.com/2024/04/03/red-wings-will-miss-rasmussen-lalonde-fischer-copp/)
- Inside The Rink, Feb 2024: "Rasmussen's offensive ability, limited though it may be, led Lalonde to move him up the lineup to play with Dylan Larkin and Lucas Raymond on the Red Wings' first line." Copp-Fischer-Rasmussen line 5v5 goal share 56%. (URL above)

### Todd McLellan (Dec 26, 2024 to present)
- Feb 24, 2025 deployment (Free Press): "Rasmussen (nine goals, seven assists in 56 games) centered the third line, played net-front on the second power-play unit and helped out killing penalties." (Helene St. James, https://www.freep.com/story/sports/nhl/red-wings/2025/02/24/detroit-red-wings-injury-news-michael-rasmussen-andrew-copp/80040456007/)
- May 10, 2025 season review: "Well, he's pretty trusting player. You can put him on the ice in a lot of different situations. Penalty kill, power play. He's been able to check. He's been able to play center or wing. So he kind of does a little bit of everything. He's probably never going to score 40, but he's going to get you 10 or 12 consistently. And that's just what he is, a consistent, steady, good teammate. And we appreciate him for that." Duff's own framing: "Rasmussen is the proverbial Swiss Army Knife, in that he can and is willing to fill whatever role the team needs... He's comfortable on the wing and serviceable as a third or fourth-line center." Also: "Attempts to utilize his big body as a net-front presence on the Red Wings power play have proven to be mostly futile." (DHN, Bob Duff, https://detroithockeynow.com/red-wings-review-michael-rasmussen/)
- Oct 25-26, 2025, first healthy scratch: "I'd rather have four lines you could roll out and get a sense of rhythm. We have tough decisions to make. We think very highly of Ras and his contributions to the team. But we made a decision, and it was hard on him, and he'll bounce back." Pre-game: "Our penalty kill is drastically improved, and he's a big part of it. We gave him that assignment at the beginning of the year, so he's done a really good job in that area... we're quite happy with what he's done there in those situations." / "Five on five, we've asked him to play the wing and center, he's moved around from line to line. A little bit of a utility guy. Like our team, he's had some really good nights and some average nights. As you get bumps and bruises, and if he's not included in that list, there has to be more from him all over the ice surface and we've talked about it. But the real positive is his contribution on the penalty kill." TOI at that point: 11:40, third-lowest on the team. (THN, Michael Whitaker, https://thehockeynews.com/nhl/detroit-red-wings/players/todd-mclellan-confident-michael-rasmussen-will-respond-the-right-way-after-scratch, 2025-10-26)
- Nov 13-15, 2025, after a 6-3 win over Anaheim: "I thought Ras played his best game of the season... for a lot of different reasons. He looked confident, he looked big, strong, held on to pucks, and was physical. So for him, that's a real big confidence booster." (DHN, Bob Duff, https://detroithockeynow.com/2025/11/15/rasmussen-woes-symbolic-of-overall-red-wings-malady-mclellan/, rest paywalled)
- Mar 2026: McLellan's "We have a lot of guys that are just jerseys on the ice" post-game line (not aimed at Rasmussen by name) was applied to him by Octopus Thrower and fans. (https://octopusthrower.com/the-red-wings-need-to-have-a-serious-conversation-about-former-first-round-pick-01kkep7mbjfk, 2026-03-11)
- Apr 8, 2026: "Yeah, I think he's probably going to miss a few more games... he blocked the shot basically in the exact same spot, so I don't know if he's starting over or not." Rasmussen: "You always want to be out there supporting and helping how you can. It's hard watching." (DHN, https://detroithockeynow.com/red-wings-rasmussen-done-for-season/)
- May 30, 2026 (what the coaches want): "To be effective, Rasmussen must be playing a heavy, hard, direct game. He needs to be driving the net. He should be a physical presence... Defensively, the coaches have to be able to trust him, especially on the penalty kill. 'That's what we need from Ras,' Detroit coach Todd McLellan confirmed." (DHN, Bob Duff, https://detroithockeynow.com/red-wings-report-card-rasmussen-in-regression/)
- Octopus Thrower, Jan 8, 2026, on the mid-season rebound: "He is also a staple of the Detroit penalty kill, helping it improve from 32nd last year (70.11 percent) to 14th this year (80.7 percent)." Nine points in 19 games from Nov 28; first Detroit SHG in 78 games (Jan 5, 2026 vs OTT). (https://octopusthrower.com/michael-rasmussen-s-turnaround-deserves-more-attention)

### Position / line history in one place
- 2018-19: center and wing as a rookie; net-front on PP1/PP2 (led team in PPG in Nov 2018).
- 2019-20: Detroit "want[ed] Rasmussen at center" in Grand Rapids (fan-thread title, Sept 2019); Bultman deck: "honing his defensive game at center."
- 2021-22: Blashill shifts him to LW (Jan and late Feb 2022) to "free him up" at net front; team still "see[s] him as big checking center."
- 2022-23: 3C/checking center and top-four PK forward under Lalonde; some top-six time.
- 2023-24: 4C, checking-line wing, spot duty on Larkin's line; PK. The Copp-Fischer-Rasmussen identity line. Alternate captain (WIIM: "he has donned the 'A'").
- 2024-25: 3C, net-front on PP2, PK (Freep). TOI 13:25.
- 2025-26: "utility guy," wing and center, 4th line; PK assignment given "at the beginning of the year"; TOI 12:39; two healthy scratches.

## Part 2: Media and analytics grades

### Detroit Hockey Now (Bob Duff), the beat that has covered him most
- 2024-01-10: "he is who he is... what he is isn't all that bad." Solid/reliable/dependable/trustworthy; will not be a top-six regular or a PP net-front presence. (URL above)
- 2025-05-10 season review (2024-25): "certainly not a star," "Swiss Army Knife," "loses a lot of puck battles along the boards and is too easily knocked off his feet," could be "a player the Red Wings could package in a deal." (URL above)
- 2025-10-23 "Is it Time For Red Wings to End Rasmussen Experiment?": "many hockey people cringed" at the pick; "a big player who doesn't play big"; 6.6 hits/60 vs. rookie Brandsegg-Nygard's 13.5; 10 of last 37 goals into empty nets; 50.3 SAT% (14th on team); 5.18 blocks/60 leads forwards; 11:35 TOI. "The Rasmussen experiment has clearly run its course." (https://detroithockeynow.com/2025/10/23/is-it-time-for-red-wings-to-end-rasmussen-experiment-mclellan/)
- 2026-05-30 report card: Grade D. "Big Ras was a big mess." Shots, faceoff %, TOI all down three straight years; 12:39 TOI lowest since rookie year; -22 over two seasons vs. +8 in 2023-24; "it might be time for the Red Wings to find a way to move on." Suggested trade partners: LA (Holland) and Vancouver. (URL above)

### The Hockey Writers (Tony Wolak), microstat-based grades (data: All Three Zones, HockeyStatCards, MoneyPuck, NST, PuckIQ)
- 2024-25: Grade D+. Role: "Third-line forward with penalty kill role and lineup versatility." "He recovered dump-ins well and had decent passing microstats... much to be desired when it comes to Rasmussen's physicality and decision-making. And like Petry above, he struggled to kill penalties effectively." Preseason projection 74 GP 14-19-33; actual 77 GP 11-10-21. (https://thehockeywriters.com/detroit-red-wings-player-grades-2024-25/, 2025-04-25)
- 2025-26: Grade D. Role: "Bottom-six energy forward with penalty kill responsibilities." "some slight year-over-year improvement in his offensive microstats and he blocked more shots than any other Red Wings forward, but the cons outweighed the pros. Rasmussen had the 29th-worst Offensive Rating league-wide and had awful zone exit numbers, all playing a sheltered bottom-six role. He wasn't overly physical, either. It's unclear if he has a future in Detroit." Projection 65 GP 9-13-22; actual 64 GP 6-8-14. (https://thehockeywriters.com/red-wings-player-grades-2025-26/, 2026-04-22)
- Contract reaction, 2024-02-20 (Wolak): "tremendous value," compared favorably to Adam Lowry's 5 x $3.25M; "a big win for both the Red Wings and Rasmussen." (https://thehockeywriters.com/red-wings-michael-rasmussen-4-year-extension/)
