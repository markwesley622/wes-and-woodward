# Coverage ledger — Söderblom trade (pulled 2026-09-08)

## The trade
- **2026-03-06** DET → PIT: Elmer Söderblom for San Jose's 2026 3rd-round pick (via PIT). Sources: NHL.com/Penguins release; Yahoo/Detroit News ("Red Wings deal Elmer Soderblom to Pittsburgh for third-round pick"); Puckpedia trade 1102.
- Pre-trade context (Yahoo/DetNews): healthy scratch the previous three games (Feb 28, Mar 2, Mar 4); 16 games without a point since the Dec 20 goal at WSH; 2-1-3 in 39 GP.
- **2026-03-08** the pick was rerouted: DET acquired Justin Faulk from STL for Justin Holl, Dmitri Buchelnikov, DET 2026 1st, and the SJS 2026 3rd (Puckpedia trade 1107; NHL.com). Faulk: 33, $6.5M AAV through 2026-27, 11-21-32 in 61 GP for STL at the time.
- **2026-06-26** STL traded picks 73 and 76 to TOR for Brandon Carlo. TOR used No. 73 on Zach Olsen (C, Saskatoon WHL, 18-16-34 in 57 GP; Daily Faceoff mock had him 50th). So the Söderblom pick became Olsen, a Leafs prospect, two trades later.
- Faulk with DET: 17 GP, 5-3-8, 20:15 TOI, -5 (NHL API game log, Mar 8 to Apr 15). DET went 9-15-5 over the final 29 and missed the playoffs (10th straight year); finished 41-31-10, 92 pts, 6th Atlantic. PIT 41-25-16, 98 pts, 2nd Metro, lost R1 in six to PHI.

And we feel we have players that if it's in that 13th forward role, we can fill that role from within."

## Quotes — Pittsburgh side
- **Dubas, March 2026** (via PensBurgh season review, May 26): "I think the things that have stood out [about] him throughout this year have been just really the forecheck. Get in on the forecheck, disrupt pucks, win pucks back. ... we obviously have got the line of [Connor] Dewar, [Blake] Lizotte and [Noel Acciari] that do a really good job of that, but being able to add a little bit more of that throughout the lineup would be good."
